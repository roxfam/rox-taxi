# Backlinks Strategy — Rox Taxi Service and Tours

Backlinks (other reputable sites linking to roxtaxi.com) are the #1 off-page
SEO signal. Google ranks Rox Taxi higher for "Nassau taxi" the more travel /
tourism / cruise sites link to it. Goal: **10 high-quality backlinks in 60
days**. Total time investment: ~4 hours across a few evenings.

Every step is completely free.

---

## Tier 1 — Tourism directories (2 hours, guaranteed inclusions)

These are the highest-authority Bahamas travel sites. Every Rox Taxi listing
here counts as a strong `DA 60+` backlink.

### 1. Bahamas Ministry of Tourism (BMOT) directory — biggest single win

- URL: <https://www.bahamas.com/vendors/join>
- What: Official government directory of licensed tourism operators
- Time: 20 min to complete + 3-5 day review
- What to send:
  - Business name: Rox Taxi Service and Tours
  - Category: Ground Transportation → Taxi / Tour Operator
  - Website: https://roxtaxi.com
  - Phone: +1 (242) 432-2587
  - Description: (use the 750-char business description from GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE.md)
  - Attach: your Bahamas taxi licence + insurance certificate scan
- **This link is worth 20+ backlinks from lesser sites.** Priority #1.

### 2. TripAdvisor Bahamas — taxi / tours listing

- URL: <https://www.tripadvisor.com/GetListedNew>
- Set up as: **Transportation** (primary) + **Tours** (secondary)
- Add photos, prices, service list — reuse content from your Google Business Profile
- **Critical**: link back to `https://roxtaxi.com/travel-to-nassau` from your TripAdvisor "Website" field — this passes the most SEO juice to your new content page
- Once live, tell your 5 recent happy customers to leave a TripAdvisor review — 3-5 fresh reviews launches you into the "Trending" carousel

### 3. Nassau Paradise Island official tourist board

- URL: <https://www.nassauparadiseisland.com/business-directory> (or email `info@nassauparadiseisland.com`)
- Free listing for licensed Bahamas businesses
- Ask specifically to be listed under "Getting Around → Taxi & Tours"

### 4. Bahamas Chamber of Commerce (BCCEC)

- URL: <https://www.thebahamaschamber.com/membership>
- Membership tier: "Silver" (~$300/year) includes a directory listing + `bccec.com` backlink
- Optional but powerful — the Chamber's site has DA 55+

### 5. Better Business Bureau (BBB) Caribbean

- URL: <https://www.bbb.org/caribbean/get-accredited>
- Free basic listing. Adds a "BBB Accredited" trust badge you can put on your site (huge for conversion, not just SEO).

## Tier 2 — Cruise / travel forums (2 hours, sticky posts)

Cruise-line forums have some of the most active travel communities on the
internet. A helpful, well-written "trip report" that mentions Rox Taxi
naturally is worth 5x a generic directory listing.

### 6. Cruise Critic — Bahamas boards

- URL: <https://boards.cruisecritic.com/forum/33-caribbean/>
- Sign up (free), post 2-3 helpful replies WITHOUT linking (build karma), then post a "**Just back from Nassau — here's how I handled the cruise port to Atlantis run**" trip report.
- Include: real fares, timing, and a sentence at the end: "We prebooked Rox Taxi at roxtaxi.com — driver was waiting at the wharf with a name sign, $18 for two people, tolls included."
- **Do NOT spam.** One authentic trip report per forum, 2-3 months apart.

### 7. Reddit — r/cruise + r/BahamasTravel + r/travel

- Answer 3-5 taxi/tour questions per week. Only mention Rox Taxi when it's genuinely the best answer.
- Include a link ONLY when someone specifically asks "who did you use?"
- Reddit backlinks are `nofollow` but they drive real referral traffic (which Google counts as a positive signal).

### 8. TripAdvisor Nassau Forum

- URL: <https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowForum-g147416-i94-Nassau_New_Providence_Island_Bahamas.html>
- Same play as Reddit — answer questions, be helpful, mention Rox Taxi only when relevant. Every post is a semi-permanent traffic source.

### 9. Facebook Groups (7 to target)

Join these, be genuinely helpful for 2 weeks, then answer transportation questions with a link:
- "Nassau Bahamas Travel Tips"
- "Atlantis Paradise Island Bahamas"
- "Baha Mar Resort Bahamas"

Facebook links are `nofollow` but drive high-intent referral traffic.
