## Creative direction — what image / video to attach
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Facebook rewards **scroll-stopping visuals** with cheaper impressions.
Rank in this order:

1. **15-second phone video** — you shot from the driver seat driving
   over Paradise Island bridge with Atlantis in frame. Add subtitles
   ("From $47 · Book online at roxtaxi.com").
2. **Bright single image** — turquoise water + a Rox Taxi vehicle in
   frame. Overlay one 4-word promise: "Fixed fares. No haggling."
3. **Carousel of 3-5 images** — one per service: airport transfer,
   Blue Lagoon tour, Atlantis drop-off, rental car, wedding party.

**Avoid:** stock photos (FB penalizes generic imagery), busy text overlays
(FB now shows warnings above 20% text coverage).
