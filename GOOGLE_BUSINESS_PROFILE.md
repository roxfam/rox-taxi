**Why this matters:** For 60% of "Nassau taxi" searches Google shows a 3-listing
map pack ABOVE the regular results. Businesses in that map pack get ~10× the
clicks of the #1 organic result below it. A well-optimised Google Business
Profile (GBP) is the single biggest ranking win still available to Rox Taxi —
free, one-time setup, ~30 minutes total.

> **Already have a GBP?** Great — skip Section 1 (Claim / verify) and start at

## 3. Add photos (5 min — LARGEST single ranking factor after category)

Profiles with 100+ photos get 2.7× more direction requests than profiles with fewer than 10. Upload:

- **Logo**: your gold R monogram from `/app/frontend/public/logo-mark.png`
- **Cover**: hero photo of a Rox Taxi van at LPIA arrivals or in front of Atlantis
- **Interior**: 3-5 shots of the van interior (clean, spacious, luggage room)
- **Exterior**: van with branding at 3 different Nassau landmarks (LPIA sign, Paradise Island bridge, Bay Street, Baha Mar entrance, cruise port)
- **Team**: 3-5 driver headshots (with their permission) — Google prioritises "face" content
- **Tour photos**: reuse the best 20 from `/gallery` — one per tour (Blue Lagoon, Atlantis, Rose Island, jet ski, ATV, etc.)

Aim for **50 photos in the first month, then 3-5 new photos per week**. Google's algorithm rewards freshness.

## 4. Get 5 reviews in the first 7 days (5 min)

New listings with 5+ reviews rank ~40% higher within a month. Send this exact text via WhatsApp/email to your 5 best recent customers:

> Hi [Name] — thanks so much for riding with Rox Taxi! I just launched our Google Business Profile and would be super grateful if you could leave a quick review here: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]. Even one sentence helps small Bahamas-owned businesses like ours get seen. Thank you!

Get your review link:
1. Log into <https://business.google.com>
2. Home → click **"Ask for reviews"**
3. Copy the short link (looks like `https://g.page/r/…/review`)
4. Send to customers via WhatsApp — the link opens directly to the review form

Reply to EVERY review (positive or negative) within 24 hours. Google's algorithm sees engaged owners as more trustworthy.

## 5. Post weekly updates (2 min/week — biggest freshness signal)

Every Monday, log into GBP → **Add Update** and post one of:
- New tour launched (with photo)
- Seasonal promo (paste "SUMMER10 · 10% off through Aug 31" with a photo)
- Behind-the-scenes (driver of the week, van getting cleaned, etc.)
- Answer a common question ("What time should I leave for the cruise?")

Google shows the latest 3 posts inside your map-pack card. Fresh posts = higher ranking.
